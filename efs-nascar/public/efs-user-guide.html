💡 How Bonuses Work
  • Stage wins: Any of your 3 drivers wins Stage 1 or 2

✅ Making & Viewing Picks
📝 How to Submit Picks
1

Navigate to Picks Page

Click "Picks" in the left sidebar menu

2

Select the Race

The next upcoming race is shown by default. Use the dropdown to change races.

3

Choose 3 Drivers

Search by name or car number. Click a driver to add them to your lineup.

4

Submit Before Deadline

Watch the countdown timer! Picks lock at race start time.

💡 Pro Tips
  • You can edit/delete picks anytime before deadline
  • Watch driver usage - shows "X/4" next to each driver
  • Check Driver Rankings for hot performers
  • Look at track type when selecting drivers
👁️ Viewing Race Picks

After the deadline passes (or all teams submit), you can see everyone's picks with overlap indicators.

Pick Popularity Badges:

🔒 Pick Visibility Rules

  • Popularity badges help track contrarian vs chalk picks

⛽ Pick Strategies (Pit Stop Terminology)

Each week your 3-driver lineup is classified by a "pit strategy" based on the driver tiers you pick. Think of it like NASCAR pit stops - aggressive strategies burn more resources, conservative ones save for later.

🔥 AGGRESSIVE
Hail Melon 3 T1
All Gas 2T1+1T2
Drafting 3 T2
⚖️ BALANCED
Saving 2T1+1T3
Gas 'N' Go 1 each
Full Fuel 2T2+1T3
🧊 CONSERVATIVE
2 Tires 1T1+2T3
4 Tires 1T2+2T3
4 Tires & Fuel 3 T3
📊 Strategy Analytics
  • Your Team page shows strategy breakdown over the season
  • See avg points earned per strategy vs league average
  • Track how often you use each strategy type
💡 Strategic Thinking
  • Aggressive: High ceiling, burns T1/T2 uses fast
  • Balanced: Sustainable, good mix of upside/conservation
  • Conservative: Saves resources but lower expected points

📊 Understanding Efficiency
  • Max Potential: Points if you picked their best X races (X = times used)
  • Efficiency: Actual pts / Max potential × 100
  • High efficiency = you timed your picks well!
